Legal & Compliance - Contract Lifecycle Portal FAQ

Document ID: NCS-LC-FAQ-003 | Version: 5.0 | Effective Date: 15 August 2026

How do I request a contract review?

Raise a review request from the Contract Lifecycle Portal, attach the draft or deal summary, and select the counterparty risk tier. Requests are triaged daily by the Legal Operations team.

How do I request an urgent contract review?

Mark the request as "Urgent" in the portal and attach your department head's approval note. Urgent requests are triaged within one business hour of submission.

What are the standard and expedited turnaround times?

A standard review takes five (5) business days from triage. An expedited review takes 48 hours (two business days) from triage, and requires the approval described above.

What approvals are required for contract deviations?

Any deviation from standard terms requires Legal approval and Deal Desk sign-off. Discounts above 15 percent additionally require CFO approval.
